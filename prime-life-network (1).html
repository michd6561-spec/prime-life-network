Common questions

Is this a salary job?

No. It is commission-based. Booking Partners earn 5% and Associates earn 10% on sales, and Associates can also earn the $50 reward described above.

Who can join?

Booking Partners are people who already have a job. Associates are people willing to go through training. Both should live in or near Ikeja.

When is the Open House?

Every Monday at 10:00 AM at our office: 11 Bayode Oluwole Street, off Balogun, Ikeja. Pick the date in the form above.

What happens at the Open House?

You learn the details of the role you picked, including how qualified families are counted, and you can ask questions.
